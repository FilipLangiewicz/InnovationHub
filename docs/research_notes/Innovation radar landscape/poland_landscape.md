# Polish landscape: initiatives, institutions, platforms and programs that find, assess, transfer or replicate public-sector innovations (focus: Podkarpackie)

Research date: 21 November 2026. Scope: Poland only. All facts have a source link. Items that I could not confirm are in the "Gaps" sections. "Inferences" are my own conclusions from the cited facts.

---

## 1. Databases and platforms of good practices and innovations for local governments (JST)

### Takeaway
Poland has many small, separate JST good-practice channels: one long-lived database (Baza Dobrych Praktyk, ZMP), a few annual competitions (PAP "Innowacyjny Samorząd", FRDL, ZMP "Samorządowy Lider Zarządzania"), a 2026 replication program with a fixed catalogue of 30 social innovations (FFW "Innowacje w samorządzie") and new GovTech capacity programs (GovTech@JST, HackNation). None of them gives cross-sector search, transparent scoring, similarity detection or matching of creators with adopters.

### Cited Findings

**Baza Dobrych Praktyk (BDP), dobrepraktyki.pl (ZMP)**
- BDP is a joint local-government initiative started in 2007 by national JST associations. It collects standardised descriptions of good practices in public-service management and institutional development, and since 2013 also inter-municipal cooperation. — [BDP history](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/strona/11,historia-bdp.html)
- Over several years BDP collected "ponad 500 wystandaryzowanych [...] opisów praktyk". These older records are now in the archive at archiwum.dobrepraktyki.pl. — [BDP history](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/strona/11,historia-bdp.html); [archive](https://archiwum.dobrepraktyki.pl)
- Sources of records: winners of JST competitions (especially "Samorządowy Lider Zarządzania"), "Grupy Wymiany Doświadczeń", the offices of JST associations, local-government media and recommendations by other JST. — [BDP history](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/strona/11,historia-bdp.html)
- Funding history: Norwegian Financial Mechanism (2007-2010), POKL 5.4 and 5.2 projects (2010-2013+), and since April 2019 the project "System Monitorowania Usług Publicznych" (SMUP, POWER 2.18). ZMP coordinates the base since 2020. — [BDP history](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/strona/11,historia-bdp.html)
- The new (SMUP) BDP is live in 2026 (footer "© 2006-2026"). Categories: Finanse JST (Optymalizacja wydatków); Usługi społeczne (Edukacja, Kultura i rekreacja, Polityka społeczna); Usługi techniczne (Budownictwo, Geodezja, Nieruchomości, Ochrona środowiska, Transport); Zarządzanie (Przestrzeń publiczna, Zarządzanie danymi). Example records: Pleszew housing policy, Lublin pay analysis, Kraków TELE-BUS, Słupsk Bałtycka Biblioteka Cyfrowa. — [BDP SMUP list](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/projekt/smup/lista)
- The SMUP list has 13 pages. Page 1 shows 4 records. Thus the new base probably has about 50 records (my estimate from the pagination, not a published number). The page shows no keyword search, no scoring, no export and no API. — [BDP SMUP list](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl/projekt/smup/lista); [BDP home](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl)
- BDP links to the SMUP indicator tool at smup.gov.pl. — [BDP home](https://www.dobrepraktyki.pl)

### Inferences
- The JST good-practice field is "publish and award", not "discover, score and transfer". Each channel holds its own records (BDP, PAP, FRDL, FFW catalogue). A tool that indexes these sources and detects duplicates has a clear gap to fill.
- The FFW catalogue shows the replication model that KOWR-like or PCI-like users accept: a fixed list of proven innovations plus a grant to adopt one. A "Radar" can produce candidate entries for such catalogues from other sources.
- BDP and competition descriptions follow a standard structure (problem, solution, results). This structure can be the schema for submissions in the prototype.
- GovTech@JST (60 JST) and HackNation are channels to reach JST teams that already think about innovation; they are possible pilot users.

### Gaps
- Exact number of records in the new BDP and in archiwum.dobrepraktyki.pl today; whether BDP has an export or API (no evidence of one).
- Status in 2025-2026 of "Samorządowy Lider Zarządzania", Forum Rozwoju Lokalnego and CWD Plus.
- I did not research "Smart City Awards", the "Rzeczpospolita" Ranking Samorządów ("Lider innowacji"), ZPP's own rankings or the GovTech Polska challenge archive (number of challenges, open data).
- Content of the PARP GovTech design report (2025): barriers of JST, any plan for a JST innovation database.

## 2. Platforms and mechanisms for school innovations

### Takeaway
Polish law makes pedagogical innovation a normal school activity but, since 2017, does not require schools to notify the kurator. Thus no national register exists. Some regions collect data anyway: Kuratorium Oświaty we Wrocławiu collected 2,895 innovation records from 547 schools (2024) and found that only 16.1% had an evaluation. National channels (ORE, FRSE/eTwinning, MEN contests, Laboratoria Przyszłości stories) promote practices but do not offer a searchable, scored base.

### Cited Findings

**Legal basis**
- Since 1 September 2017 innovation is an integral part of school activity in the law (school runs "działalność dydaktyczną, wychowawczą, opiekuńczą i innowacyjną"). The law (Prawo oświatowe; the guide cites art. 68 ust. 1 pkt 9, art. 86 ust. 1-2, art. 98 ust. 2 pkt 3) removed the formal preconditions and "konieczność zgłaszania innowacji pedagogicznej kuratorowi oświaty i organowi prowadzącemu". Each school decides how to introduce and document innovations (director's order or resolution of the rada pedagogiczna). — [A. Matusiewicz, CEN Suwałki guide (2020)](https://cen.suwalki.pl/pdf/A.Matusiewicz_Innowacje_pedagogiczne_formalnosci_krok_po_kroku.pdf)
- Conflicting interpretation: an eTwinning seminar presentation says the director decides on innovations (art. 68 ust. 1 pkt 2, art. 55 ust. 1 pkt 4) and lists entry in a school register as a step. — [eTwinning seminar PDF](https://konferencje.etwinning.pl/storage/seminar/202404/48RZAeHvHfM8YrMJUrjaC4nvKSDXTUBK5oB5Cmn8.pdf)
- Pedagogical experiments (art. 45 Prawo oświatowe) are different: they need a consent. — [Kuratorium Warszawa guide](https://www.kuratorium.waw.pl/pl/poradnik-klienta/dla-dyrektorow-szkolpl/8426,Wprowadzanie-eksperymentow-pedagogicznych.pdf); [lexlege art. 45](https://lexlege.pl/prawo-oswiatowe/art-45/)
- A pedagogical innovation is "każde nowatorskie rozwiązanie programowe, organizacyjne lub metodyczne" (secondary source, 2026 teaching material). — [MSCDN material](https://mscdn.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Materialy-dydaktyczne-z-zakresu-innowacje-pedagogiczne.pdf)
- A Minister of Education regulation amending pedagogical supervision (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1151) is in force from 1 September 2026; it brings back "monitorowanie" as a form of supervision. — [eli.gov.pl](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/1151/ogl/pol); [portaloswiatowy.pl](https://www.portaloswiatowy.pl/zmiany-w-prawie-oswiatowym/nadzor-pedagogiczny-od-1-wrzesnia-wraca-monitorowanie-27749.html)

**Regional registers and reports**
- Report "Innowacje edukacyjne w dolnośląskich szkołach" (Wrocław, January 2026; Zespół ds. badań przy Wojewódzkim Zespole Koordynacji): online questionnaire Sept-Oct 2024, distributed by Kuratorium Oświaty we Wrocławiu; N = 2,895 innovation records from 547 schools (identified by RSPO). — [DODN report PDF](https://dodn.dolnyslask.pl/wp-content/uploads/1.-Innowacje-edukacyjne-w-dolnoslaskich-szkolach.pdf)
- Key numbers from that report:
  - Innovations per school: 1 innovation = 23.8% of schools; 2-3 = 28.7%; 4-6 = 21.6%; 7-10 = 12.4%; 11+ = 13.5%.
  - Type: mixed 50.5%, methodical 31.9%, curricular 14.2%, organisational 3.4%.
  - Stage: primary grades 4-8 = 37.4%; grades 1-3 = 35.3%; secondary = 10.1%.
  - 81.9% are linked to one subject; median scale 30 participants; median duration about 277 days; about 1.9% of records have date errors.
  - Declared evaluation: yes 16.1%, no 83.9%.
  - Source for all: [DODN report](https://dodn.dolnyslask.pl/wp-content/uploads/1.-Innowacje-edukacyjne-w-dolnoslaskich-szkolach.pdf)
- The report recommends a "Karta innowacji jako standard minimum", light but real evaluation, a school repository with a common language of innovation, and transfer mechanisms (networks, mentoring, repository, "warunki wdrożenia") with leader schools as diffusion nodes. — [DODN report](https://dodn.dolnyslask.pl/wp-content/uploads/1.-Innowacje-edukacyjne-w-dolnoslaskich-szkolach.pdf)
- Podkarpackie: the 2024/2025 supervision report of Kuratorium Oświaty w Rzeszowie has template sections for conclusions on "innowacyjnych działań programowych, organizacyjnych lub metodycznych" and mentions spreading good practices (e.g. career guidance), but I found no register of innovations. 351 planned inspections were done in 2024/2025. — [KO Rzeszów report 2024/2025](https://www.ko.rzeszow.pl/_wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/sprawozdanie_pko_-2024_2025.pdf)
- KO Rzeszów publishes the bulletin "Meritum" with school examples. — [Meritum nr 34](https://www.ko.rzeszow.pl/pliki/meritum/meritum_nr_34.pdf)
- Teacher-training centres keep small good-practice bases, e.g. ŚCDN Kielce "Baza dobrych praktyk" with publication rules. — [ŚCDN baza](https://samorzad.gov.pl/web/scdn-kielce/baza-dobrych-praktyk); [ŚCDN rules](https://samorzad.gov.pl/web/scdn-kielce/regulamin-publikowania-przykladow-dobrych-praktyk)

**National institutions and programs**
- ORE (Ośrodek Rozwoju Edukacji) lists "upowszechnianie dobrych praktyk" among its tasks. In 2026 ORE ran a competition to select upper-secondary schools for a pilot of modular e-textbooks (deadline 30 April 2026), which also collects good practices. I found no ORE good-practice database. — [ORE plan 2026](https://bip.ore.edu.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/plan-dzialanosci-ORE-2026.pdf); [gov.pl, e-textbook pilot](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/wybor-szkoly-ponadpodstawowej-do-pilotazu-wdrazania-modulowych-e-podrecznikow--konkurs-osrodka-rozwoju-edukacji)
- MEN contest "Kierunek – innowacja" for teachers: editions 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 (2024 deadline 26 July 2024); results list winners by voivodeship, including Podkarpackie. — [2021 results](https://www.gov.pl/web/nauka/konkurs-dla-nauczycieli-szkol-podstawowych-i-ponadpodstawowych-kierunek--innowacja--znamy-wyniki); [2024 call](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/konkurs-dla-nauczycieli-kierunek--innowacja-2024); [2024 results](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/konkurs-dla-nauczycieli-przedszkoli-szkol-podstawowych-i-ponadpodstawowych-kierunek--innowacja-2024--znamy-wyniki)
- FRSE runs the National eTwinning Bureau. In 20 years eTwinning connected 1.2 million teachers from 295,000 schools in 46 countries (European total, no Polish split given). - The Zintegrowana Platforma Edukacyjna (zpe.gov.pl) has a base of trainings, events and projects for teachers ("Rozwijaj się cyfrowo"). — [ZPE](https://zpe.gov.pl/a/rozwijaj-sie-cyfrowo-baza-szkolen-wydarzen-i-projektow-dla-nauczycieli/D3E4LQLMS)
- Fundacja Szkoła z Klasą has a live site in 2026. — [szkolazklasa.org.pl](https://www.szkolazklasa.org.pl/) (content not analysed)
- Podkarpackie teacher-training centre (PCEN, Rzeszów/Przemyśl) exists; I found no PCEN innovation base or contest. — [gov.pl, PCEN Przemyśl](https://www.gov.pl/web/nauka/spotkanie-w-podkarpackim-centrum-edukacji-nauczycieli-w-przemyslu-z-udzialem-ministra-przemyslawa-czarnka)

### Inferences
- School innovations are the most "hidden" group: they are numerous (2,895 records in one region), small (median 30 participants), short (one school year) and rarely evaluated (16.1%). This fits the "Radar" task exactly.
- The Wrocław questionnaire fields (type, area, stage, subject, scale, duration, evaluation; RSPO ID) are a tested minimum schema. The prototype can reuse them for school submissions and add creators, results and transfer conditions.
- Because no legal obligation to notify exists, discovery must rely on voluntary submission, school websites and BIP, kuratorium reports, contest winner lists and eTwinning/Erasmus+ project lists.
- In Podkarpackie, a partnership with Kuratorium Oświaty w Rzeszowie (supervision now includes monitoring from 1 Sept 2026) and PCEN is a realistic channel to collect records.

### Gaps
- Whether Kuratorium Oświaty w Rzeszowie or other kuratoria (besides Wrocław) collect innovation records, and whether the Wrocław data is open.
- CEO (Centrum Edukacji Obywatelskiej) programs and "Szkoła z klasą" content; FRSE Erasmus+ project result data for Poland (the EU Erasmus+ Project Results Platform is covered by the EU researcher).
- Status of "Kierunek – innowacja" in 2025-2026 and of Laboratoria Przyszłości in 2026.
- Number of Polish schools and teachers registered in eTwinning.

## 3. Platforms and programs for universities and research (technology transfer)

### Takeaway
The research side has the best data: the state RAD-on open API (OPI PIB) exposes about 48,900 research projects with abstracts and keywords, free and without a key. Technology offers of CTTs and Łukasiewicz institutes are mostly PDF catalogues, not searchable bases. Commercialisation money flows through Science4Business (FENG, 296.7 mln PLN), PARP Platformy startowe and regional PCI grants. In Podkarpackie, Politechnika Rzeszowska (CTT with "Wirtualny Akcelerator Komercjalizacji") and Uniwersytet Rzeszowski (CIiTWTP, Science4Business consortium) are the key actors.

### Inferences
- RAD-on projects API is the best free, legal, national data source for the "find similar solutions" function (research side): titles, PL/EN abstracts and keywords are ready for text-similarity search, and institutions allow a filter by region (Podkarpackie).
- University technology offers exist mainly as PDFs. A Radar can add value by converting these offers into structured records and linking them to JST or school needs.
- PCI's grant program already builds a pipeline of TRL 3-6 projects from Podkarpackie universities. The Radar should feed this pipeline and not compete with CTTs.

- A 2026 public tender (BZP 2026/BZP 00241631/01) concerns a monitoring report of the Podkarpackie RSI for 2024-2025 (seen on an aggregator; I did not see the original notice). — [tenderglass.com listing](https://tenderglass.com/en/poland-country-pl/research-and-development-consultancy-services-cpv-73200000)
- Other regions publish RIS monitoring reports (Lubelskie yearly; Śląskie for 2021-2022 and 2023-2024). — [Lubelskie 2024](https://lubelskie.pl/file/2023/06/RAPORT-MONITORINGOWY-2024.pdf); [Śląskie 2023-24](https://ris.slaskie.pl/resource/705/0.+2025-05-23_RIS2030_Raport+monit+2023-24_BK_bez+CP1.pdf)
- Dolina Lotnicza: an older article says the cluster regained KKK status (in the Inteligentny Rozwój 2014-2020 era). A Council of Ministers ordinance of 9 July 2026 created a "Rada Przemysłu Lotniczego"; the leader of "Stowarzyszenie Grupy Przedsiębiorców Przemysłu Lotniczego Dolina Lotnicza" sits on it. — [dlapilota.pl](https://dlapilota.pl/wiadomosci/dolina-lotnicza/dolina-lotnicza-ponownie-krajowym-klastrem-kluczowym); [defence24.pl](https://defence24.pl/przemysl/rada-przemyslu-lotniczego-powolana-przez-premiera)
- PCI's InnoStart Hub and Deeptech Akcelerator are national FENG programs, so PCI already handles ideas from outside Podkarpackie. — [InnoStart Hub](https://pcinn.org/projekty/innostart-hub/)

### Inferences
- PCI's merit card is a ready, official scoring rubric. The prototype can map each submitted idea to these criteria: R&D/problem fit, TRL, RIS fit, multidisciplinarity, competitive advantage, market need, recipient benefit, implementation path, MVP, validation with a recipient, dual-use. Jury members from PCI will recognise it.
- The PCI rubric is built for university R&D grants. For school and JST innovations the prototype needs extra criteria (social impact, cost of replication, transferability, evidence of evaluation). The Wrocław data shows that evaluation evidence is often missing.
- PCI has instruments for most "paths" that the task names:
  - university R&D with TRL 3-6: Program Grantowy;
  - researchers who want a spin-off: PAPA;
  - startups up to 5 years old with a prototype: Deeptech Akcelerator;
  - individual idea holders: InnoStart Hub;
  - young people: ProtoLab;
  - tests and calibration: PSLBiW labs;
  - partner search and consortia: Komercjalizacja and Business Speed Dating events.
  - For JST, the main external path is the FFW "Innowacje w samorządzie" grant (replication).
  - The Radar can recommend these real paths.
- PCI's public IT is a call system, a lab directory and a procurement portal. I found no public pipeline database, CRM, idea scouting tool or similarity search. A Radar would be an addition, not a replacement.

### Gaps
- No public PCI document about a "laboratory for innovators aged 16-30": I could not confirm the age range. ProtoLab targets pupils, students and young entrepreneurs.
- Details of DeCo, Mount Resilience, the Deeptech Akcelerator selection criteria and the InnoStart Hub "ocena innowacyjności" method.
- Number of laboratories in PSLBiW; detail fields of lab records.
- Aeropolis (Podkarpacki Park Naukowo-Technologiczny, RARR), Regionalne Obserwatorium Terytorialne of Podkarpackie, the RSI entrepreneurial discovery process (working groups) and any regional innovation monitoring dashboard: not found in my searches.
- Current KKK status of Dolina Lotnicza in 2026.

## 5. Polish tools that already do automatic discovery or matching of innovations

### Takeaway
I found no Polish tool that automatically discovers innovations in schools, JST and public institutions, scores them and matches creators with adopters. The closest items are planned or partial: the ministry's "Smart Radar 2.0" (market consultation in 2026; monitors companies and smart specialisations, with a planned matchmaking "Platforma innowacji" and "Mapy innowacji"), OPI's portal and RAD-on API (research data, no matching), the EU Innovation Radar (EU-funded projects) and static "radar" publications (KPT Kraków RAD4R).

### Cited Findings
- "Smart Radar 2.0 – system do monitorowania innowacyjności i zaawansowania technologicznego przedsiębiorstw": Ministerstwo Rozwoju i Technologii (Departament Innowacyjności i Polityki Kosmicznej) asked for offers to estimate the contract value (not a tender); co-financed by FENG; offers due 20 April 2026. — [MRiT notice (docx)](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/4f4b8c85-a966-431a-925c-d00aa963ca5a)
- The old Smart Radar was "podstawowe narzędzie do monitorowania krajowych i regionalnych inteligentnych specjalizacji". Smart Radar 2.0 should monitor:
  - applications and signed projects in KIS and RIS areas (2021-2027, with a view to 2028-2034);
  - key and critical technologies;
  - technology trends with AI.
  - It also plans two interactive modules: "platforma matchmakingowa (Platforma innowacji)" and "Mapy innowacji" (map of R&D projects filtered by KIS, RIS, technology, TRL and location).
  - Source: [MRiT notice](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/4f4b8c85-a966-431a-925c-d00aa963ca5a)
- Planned Smart Radar 2.0 data sources and terms:
  - API integration with GUS, Eurostat, NCBR, PARP, PAIH, Interreg and CORDIS, and with systems such as cst2021.gov.pl, stat.gov.pl and JEMS;
  - its own API for "Otwarte Dane";
  - transfer of copyright and source code to the ministry;
  - a 10-stage plan with a 45-working-day matchmaking stage;
  - an update of the KIS list planned for 2026.
  - Source: [MRiT notice](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/4f4b8c85-a966-431a-925c-d00aa963ca5a)
- The EU Innovation Radar identifies high-potential innovations and innovators in EU-funded research projects; it started in 2018 and reached 10,000 innovations. A Polish NCP page describes the "EU innovation platform" linked to the Innovation Radar. — [EC Innovation Radar](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/pl/policies/innovation-radar); [EC 10,000 innovations](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/pl/news/10000-eu-funded-innovations-market-potential-are-now-more-visible-and-accessible-potential); [gov.pl/hpkpz](https://www.gov.pl/web/hpkpz/eu-innovation-platform)
- "RAD4R – Radar innowacji przemysłu 4.0" (Krakowski Park Technologiczny, 2024) is a PDF selection of young firms with offers for industry, not a live tool. — [KPT RAD4R PDF](https://www.kpt.krakow.pl/app/uploads/2026/02/rad4r-innowacje-dla-przemyslu_2024.pdf)
- innowacje.opi.org.pl aggregates links to research systems; RAD-on exposes research projects through an open API (see section 3). — [innowacje.opi.org.pl](https://innowacje.opi.org.pl/); [RAD-on projects API](https://radon.nauka.gov.pl/opendata/polon/projects?resultNumbers=1)
- EU-funded project lists: MFiPR publishes a full spreadsheet list of 2014-2020 projects (data as of 30 June 2026, from SL2014) with title, short description, beneficiary, program, measure, value, EU co-financing and location. 2021-2027 lists are per program. Kohesio (EU) aggregates project lists, but not yet all 2021-2027 programs. — [gov.pl list 30.06.2026](https://www.gov.pl/web/fundusze-regiony/lista-projektow-realizowanych-z-funduszy-europejskich-2014-2020---30-czerwca-2026-roku); [Kohesio](https://kohesio.ec.europa.eu/pl/informacje)

### Inferences
- No existing Polish tool combines all six functions of the task (discover, structure, score, recommend path, detect similar work, match people) across schools, universities and JST. The prototype can position itself as the "front end" that finds and qualifies ideas and then routes them to existing instruments (PCI programs, FFW grants, GovTech@JST, CTTs).
- Smart Radar 2.0 is the nearest future competitor, but it targets companies and smart-specialisation statistics. The Radar can describe itself as complementary: public-sector and education innovations, which Smart Radar does not cover. It can also align its taxonomy with KIS/RIS, because MRiT and PCI both use RIS codes.
- Reusable data for the prototype (legal and free, as far as I can see):
  - RAD-on projects API (similarity with research projects);
  - BDP pages and contest winner lists (similarity with JST practices; check terms of use before scraping);
  - the FFW catalogue of 30 innovations (ready replication offers);
  - PCI RIS attachment 8 (taxonomy) and PCI scoring card (rubric);
  - PSLBiW directory (labs for validation);
  - EU project lists and Kohesio (funded projects).

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of a live Polish "Platforma Innowacji", "Baza Innowacji", "Mapa innowacji" or "Innovation Hub" with automatic discovery for the public sector. Absence in search results does not prove absence.
- The status of the Smart Radar 2.0 procurement after April 2026 (tender, contractor, launch date).
- Whether the old Smart Radar is still online (smartradar.gov.pl did not respond on 21 Nov 2026).
- Terms of use (license) for scraping BDP, contest pages and PCI documents.
